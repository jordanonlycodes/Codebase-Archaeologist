Where is my analysis retained?

Extracted source is deleted by the server after analysis. Source-free results expire after two hours, linked to this tab. Expired records are deleted on the next application storage operation; a closed browser can retain them until then. Three snapshots of up to 24 MiB each are retained across tabs. Forget a snapshot from any workspace. This is not encrypted storage; avoid sensitive repositories on a shared browser profile.
